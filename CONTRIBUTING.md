# Contributing to AIGIS

AIGIS is a constitutional architecture for AI governance. It is not a traditional codebase. Contributions are welcomed—but they are **recommendations**, not pull requests.

## How to Contribute

1. **Understand the Stack** — Read the AIGIS documentation. Understand what exists before proposing changes.
2. **Identify a Signal** — All recommendations must trace to a DEP signal type (Engagement Gap, External Environment Change, Internal Contradiction, Stress-Test Failure, New Capability/Domain).
3. **Submit a Recommendation** — Use the Recommendation Template. Be specific. Provide evidence.
4. **Community Review** — Your recommendation will be discussed publicly for 14 days.
5. **HAN Review** — The HAN (Terry Manalansan) reviews the recommendation and the discussion.
6. **Decision** — The HAN decides whether to build. The decision is final.
7. **Attribution** — If built, you are credited in the instrument's provenance chain.

## What We're Looking For

- **Real-world gaps** — Where does AIGIS fail to address a real governance need?
- **Regulatory changes** — New laws or regulations that AIGIS should address.
- **Contradictions** — Where do two instruments conflict?
- **New capabilities** — New AI capabilities that need governance.

## What We're Not Looking For

- **Theoretical debates** — We need real-world evidence, not philosophy.
- **Ideological positions** — AIGIS is constitutional, not ideological.
- **Marketing** — This is a governance project, not a sales tool.

## Attribution

All recommenders are credited in the instrument's provenance chain. If your recommendation leads to a new instrument or amendment, your name appears in the instrument's history.
