# AIGIS Governance

AIGIS is governed under a **"Sole Builder with Community Recommenders"** model.

## The HAN (Human Authority Node)

Terry Manalansan is the HAN and the Sole Builder of AIGIS. The HAN has final authority over all decisions regarding the AIGIS stack. This is not negotiable. AIGIS is a constitutional system—and constitutional systems have a single final authority.

## The Community Recommenders

Anyone may submit a recommendation for a new instrument or amendment. Recommendations follow the DEP signal types and use the Recommendation Template. Recommendations are reviewed publicly and discussed for 14 days before the HAN decides.

## The Core Advisors

The HAN may appoint a small group of Core Advisors—trusted experts who provide strategic recommendations. Core Advisors have no decision-making authority. They advise.

## The Known Adopters

Organizations using AIGIS voluntarily list themselves in ADOPTERS.md. Known Adopters provide real-world feedback and use cases. They have no decision-making authority. They inform.

## Decision-Making

- All decisions are made by the HAN.
- The HAN considers community recommendations, Core Advisor input, and Known Adopter feedback.
- The HAN's decision is final.

## Transparency

- All recommendations and their dispositions are public.
- All discussions are public.
- All instruments are public.

## Amendments

This governance model may be amended by the HAN at any time. Amendments are announced publicly.
