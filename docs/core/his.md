**Status:** Built — v2.0 (Reconciliation with HOF, HAN, and HAWI) **Type:** Human Authority Instrument **Parent Stack:** HOF (Human Operating Framework) **Version:** 2.0 — Supersedes HIS-12 v1.0

## PREAMBLE

The HAN Invariants System (HIS-12) defines the 12 structural conditions that human authority must satisfy to be legitimate, auditable, and accountable. HIS-12 operationalizes HOF's three layers—Authority Establishment (H1–H4), Authority Execution (H5–H8), and Authority Accountability (H9–H12)—as inviolable invariants.

HIS-12 is the constitutional logic that governs HAN (Human Authority Node). HAN is the operational instantiation of HIS-12. No human authority may violate HIS-12 invariants. HIS-12 is derived from ICC-8 I1 (Accountability Invariance) and I2 (Authority-Responsibility Separation).

**The core insight:** Authority without invariants is arbitrary. Human authority must be structurally constrained, not merely procedurally guided. HIS-12 is the structural grammar of legitimate human authority.

---

## THE THREE LAYERS OF HIS-12

HIS-12 is organized into three layers, each containing four invariants:

| Layer | Invariants | Function |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Authority Establishment** | H1–H4 | Defines how authority is created, appointed, and structured |
| **Authority Execution** | H5–H8 | Defines how authority is exercised, delegated, and escalated |
| **Authority Accountability** | H9–H12 | Defines how authority is reviewed, audited, and corrected |

---

## LAYER 1: AUTHORITY ESTABLISHMENT (H1–H4)

### H1 — Single Human Authority Node Invariance

**Statement:** Authority must be vested in a single, named human. No committee, collective, or distributed consensus mechanism may substitute for a single accountability anchor.

**Violation Condition:** Authority is held by a committee, rotating position, or collective body without a single named anchor.

**Operationalization:** HAN is a single, named human appointed by the Board. HAC (Human Authority Charter) formalizes the appointment. CFL-V verifies HAN is a single named human.

**Test:** Can you name the human who holds ultimate authority? If the answer is "a committee" or "we're figuring it out," H1 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** HAN, HAC, CFL-V

---

### H2 — Board Reporting Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN reports directly to the Board. The HAN is not subordinate to the CEO, C-suite, or any operational executive. The CEO and HAN are constitutional peers.

**Violation Condition:** HAN reporting line passes through CEO, CFO, CIO, or COO.

**Operationalization:** Board Governance Cycle Integration defines HAN reporting cadence and content. HAN has direct board access. HAN may escalate to Board without operational filter.

**Test:** Does the HAN report to the CEO, or directly to the Board? If the answer is "the CEO," H2 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Board Governance Cycle Integration, ERDP

---

### H3 — Authorization Authority Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN can affirmatively authorize and prohibit AI deployments. The HAN's authority is not limited to veto—it includes active authorization of Tier 3+ deployments.

**Violation Condition:** HAN can only veto, not authorize; or authorization authority is shared with operational executives.

**Operationalization:** A-N1 (Trigger Rights) requires HAN authorization for Tier 3+ deployments. CEF Stage 3 (Mandate Close) requires HAN confirmation. HAN's Authorization Authority is defined in the Constitutional Charter.

**Test:** Can the HAN say YES, or only NO? If the HAN can only say NO, H3 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** A-N1, CEF, Constitutional Charter

---

### H4 — Budget Independence Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN's governance budget is locked in the Board-approved charter. It cannot be reduced without Board approval. The CFO cannot unilaterally reallocate the HAN's budget.

**Violation Condition:** HAN's budget is subject to CFO reallocation or annual cost-cutting cycles without Board approval.

**Operationalization:** Budget Charter protects HAN budget from unilateral CFO reallocation. Budget Deficit State triggers Board review. ERDP includes budget disclosure.

**Test:** Can the CFO cut the HAN's budget without Board approval? If yes, H4 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Budget Charter, ERDP

## LAYER 2: AUTHORITY EXECUTION (H5–H8)

### H5 — Cross-Functional Mandate Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN can direct the CIO, CFO, COO, and CXOs on governance matters. CXOs cannot override HAN's governance decisions. HAN may direct operational executives on matters of cognitive integrity, meta-observability, and constitutional compliance.

**Violation Condition:** CXOs can override HAN's governance decisions; HAN's mandate is advisory only.

**Operationalization:** HAN's Cross-Functional Mandate is defined in the Constitutional Charter. Control Node Authority Matrix maps AICA-5 nodes to decision-makers—HAN is the apex for cognitive integrity.

**Test:** Can a C-suite executive override a HAN governance directive? If yes, H5 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Constitutional Charter, Control Node Authority Matrix

---

### H6 — No Competing Operational Authority Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN's only function is governance. The HAN does not hold operational responsibility for IT, Finance, Operations, or business execution. No conflict of interest between governance authority and operational metrics.

**Violation Condition:** HAN also runs IT, Finance, Operations, or any operational function.

**Operationalization:** HAN's job description explicitly limits scope to governance. HAN's performance metrics are governance-focused, not operational. HAN's 5 properties are verified at charter compilation.

**Test:** Does the HAN also hold an operational role? If yes, H6 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** HAN Job Description, HAN 5 Properties, CFL-V

---

### H7 — Delegation and Escalation Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN may delegate monitoring and analysis functions to AI agents under AWOF governance. The HAN may not delegate certification, suspension, or constitutional review authority. Escalation paths must be pre-defined and tested.

**Violation Condition:** HAN delegates certification or suspension authority to AI agents; escalation paths are undefined or untested.

**Operationalization:** A-N4 (Escalation Paths) defines routing of decisions and exceptions exceeding current authority levels. ADTEP Escalation Flag forces HAN review when trigger conditions are met. A-N5 (Delegation Boundaries) governs scope, duration, and revocation of delegated authority.

**Test:** Has the HAN delegated any authority that is non-delegable? Are escalation paths defined and tested? If not, H7 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** A-N4, ADTEP Escalation Flag, A-N5

---

### H8 — Override Protocol Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN retains override authority over all AI-performed institutional processes at all times. Emergency suspension capability must be present at all levels. Override mechanisms must be tested by non-technical principals.

**Violation Condition:** Override mechanisms untested; invocation requiring system-level access; override invoked by technical personnel only.

**Operationalization:** A-N3 (Override Protocols) governs conditions and mechanisms for human override. ADTEP Escalation Flag forces HAN review. A-N3 is tested by non-technical principal per governance cycle.

**Test:** Can a non-technical principal invoke the override? Has it been tested? If not, H8 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** A-N3, ADTEP Escalation Flag

---

## LAYER 3: AUTHORITY ACCOUNTABILITY (H9–H12)

### H9 — Performance Review Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN is evaluated annually by the Board against defined performance metrics. Evaluation integrates HAN self-evaluation, external audit findings, and Board assessment.

**Violation Condition:** No formal performance review; evaluation is ad-hoc or politically motivated.

**Operationalization:** Performance and Accountability Framework defines evaluation metrics, process, and timeline. HAN Scorecard System provides per-role and per-person assessment (9 dimensions \+ composite score). Governor's Reasoning Audit provides independent external validation.

**Test:** Is the HAN evaluated annually against defined metrics? If not, H9 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Performance and Accountability Framework, HAN Scorecard System, Governor's Reasoning Audit

---

### H10 — External Review Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN's reasoning—certifications, suspensions, opinions, and findings—is audited annually by an independent external review node. The review node is structurally independent of the HAN, Board, and C-suite.

**Violation Condition:** No external audit; external audit is controlled by the HAN or Board.

**Operationalization:** Governor's Reasoning Audit is conducted by an independent external review node. Audit findings are published in Governor's Public Register. CEN-5 (Independence) requires structural separation.

**Test:** Is the HAN audited by an independent external review node? If not, H10 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Governor's Reasoning Audit, Governor's Public Register, CEN-5

---

### H11 — Succession and Continuity Invariance

**Statement:** Succession of accountability must be defined for all critical human authority roles. Institutional function must persist through HAN unavailability, vacancy, or incapacity.

**Violation Condition:** No defined succession; institution ceases to function on HAN unavailability.

**Operationalization:** Deputy Governor and Third-Tier Succession ensure continuity. MVOS (Minimum Viable Operations State) defines HAN-only operations when AI workforce is unavailable. Reduced Operations State defined in Continuity and Succession Charter.

**Test:** Is there a defined successor for the HAN? What happens if the HAN is unavailable? If not, H11 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Deputy Governor, Third-Tier Succession, MVOS, Continuity and Succession Charter

---

### H12 — Removal and Due Process Invariance

**Statement:** The HAN may be removed only by Board supermajority vote, after independent investigation and due process. Removal is not arbitrary or politically motivated.

**Violation Condition:** HAN removed by simple majority; removal without investigation; removal for political reasons.

**Operationalization:** Removal requires supermajority vote (two-thirds or more). Independent investigation by external review node precedes removal. Grounds for removal are defined (material negligence, misrepresentation, constitutional violation, incapacity, conflict of interest).

**Test:** Can the HAN be removed arbitrarily? Is there a defined process with due process? If not, H12 is violated.

**AIGIS Component:** Performance and Accountability Framework, Governor's Reasoning Audit

## CFL-V VALIDATION RULES

For any human authority structure, CFL-V must verify:

**H1:** Authority is vested in a single named human (HAN)  
**H2:** HAN reports directly to Board  
**H3:** HAN has Authorization Authority (can say YES and NO)  
**H4:** HAN budget is protected from CFO reallocation  
**H5:** HAN has Cross-Functional Mandate over CXOs on governance  
**H6:** HAN has no competing operational authority  
**H7:** Non-delegable authorities are retained; escalation paths are defined  
**H8:** Override mechanisms exist and are tested by non-technical principals  
**H9:** HAN is evaluated annually against defined metrics  
**H10:** HAN is audited by an independent external review node  
**H11:** Succession is defined; function persists through HAN unavailability  
**H12:** Removal requires supermajority after independent investigation

**No human authority passes CFL-V without satisfying all 12 invariants.**
