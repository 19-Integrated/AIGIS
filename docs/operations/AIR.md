---

## PREAMBLE

The Annual Institutional Report (AIR) is the primary periodic disclosure instrument of 19 Integrated, providing external legibility of governance health under ICC-8 I8 External Legibility Invariance. It answers: *What is the state of 19 Integrated's governance architecture, and what material actions have occurred during the reporting period?*

AIR provides periodic external disclosure of governance health—satisfying the periodic disclosure cycle requirement of CEN-4 and the external legibility interface required under I8. It ensures that 19 Integrated's institutional actions are externally legible, auditable, and contestable from outside the institution.

**The core insight:** A governance structure that is never externally reported is not transparent—it is private. AIR transforms internal governance into externally verifiable institutional fact, satisfying the constitutional requirement that material institutional actions be periodically disclosed to defined external parties.

## SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

### Purpose

AIR ensures that:

1. **Governance health is externally legible** — External parties can assess governance health  
2. **Material actions are disclosed** — Material institutional actions are reported  
3. **Periodic disclosure is maintained** — Annual cadence satisfies CEN-4  
4. **Transparency is tiered** — Tier-specific versions serve different audiences  
5. **Accountability is demonstrated** — HAN attests to report accuracy  
6. **Contestation is enabled** — AIR provides basis for external contestation

### Scope

| In Scope | Out of Scope |
| :---- | :---- |
| Annual governance health disclosure | Client engagement confidentiality (CEF) |
| Tier-specific versions (T-PB, T-SH, T-RG, T-DS) | IP licensing terms (ILTP) |
| Seven sections of AIR content | Business strategy or marketing |
| HAN attestation | Operational efficiency |
| Disclosure Record logging | Technical delivery execution |

### Governing Relationships

| Instrument | Relationship to AIR |
| :---- | :---- |
| **ERDP** | Parent instrument — AIR is the primary periodic report of ERDP |
| **ICC-8** | Constitutional parent — I8 External Legibility, I6 Transparency Gradient |
| **CEN-4** | Periodic disclosure cycle — AIR satisfies CEN-4 |
| **IMP** | AIR logged as GAO; Disclosure Record logged |
| **AICA-5** | Measurement Framework feeds AIR |
| **ILTP** | IP Register status feeds AIR |
| **CGF** | Public Designation Register feeds AIR |
| **HAN/HOF** | HAN attests to report accuracy |
