# AIGIS Known Adopters

This is a list of organizations and projects that have publicly acknowledged their use of AIGIS (AI Governance Infrastructure Stack). It serves three purposes:

1. **Transparency** — Shows who is using AIGIS in production or active evaluation
2. **Community** — Helps adopters connect and share experiences
3. **Validation** — Demonstrates real-world adoption of the framework

## How to Add Your Organization

If you are using AIGIS, we'd love to hear from you! Adding your organization is simple:

1. **Fork this repository** or **open a pull request**
2. Add your organization to the appropriate section below (Production, Evaluation, or Research)
3. Include your organization's name, a brief description of how you're using AIGIS, and a link to your website (optional)
4. Submit a pull request

**No legal agreement required.** This is a voluntary, community-driven list. We do not share your data with third parties.

All additions to this file are subject to HAN review to ensure the integrity and accuracy of the Known Adopters list.
